## Full description (≤4000)

```
Split VPN is a VPN that sends only the traffic of the services you choose through an encrypted tunnel. Everything else on your phone keeps working directly, as if there were no VPN.

How it works
You tick the services that should go through the VPN: Telegram, YouTube and Google services, WhatsApp and Instagram, X (Twitter), OpenAI and Discord. The app adds system routes only for the networks of those services. Traffic to them is encrypted and goes through our server, while banking apps, websites, games, calls and everything else are left untouched.

Why it is convenient
• Does not slow down your internet: only what you selected goes through the tunnel.
• Does not waste battery: if the selected services are idle, the app has nothing to do.
• No need to switch the VPN on and off for banking, government and local sites — they always work directly.
• One screen: an on/off button, the list of services and the connection status.

No accounts, no payments
No sign-up. On first launch the app obtains server access by itself using the Google Play Integrity check. On devices without Google services you can enter an access code instead.

Stable in the background
The Autostart tab helps you disable battery optimization and allow auto-start so the system does not stop the VPN. After a reboot the VPN turns on again if it was on before.

Privacy
No accounts and no personal data. Traffic content is never decrypted or stored. The server keeps only a short technical connection log for debugging, deleted within 24 hours. There are no ads, analytics or third-party trackers in the app. See the privacy policy for details.

About the VPN
The app uses Android VpnService to route traffic of the selected services through an encrypted tunnel to our server. Traffic of other apps does not go through the tunnel.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
```
